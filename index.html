Section III

Research Areas

I

Multilingual Singing Voice Synthesis

Phonetic representations and cross-lingual learning for singing synthesis, with an emphasis on pronunciation modeling and low-resource languages.

II

Generative Music & Audio Editing

Models that generate, transform, and reinterpret music while preserving controllable aspects of musical content, style, and production.

III

Cognitive Science

Computational and experimental perspectives on human memory, attention, perception, and learning — and how these mechanisms shape interaction with intelligent systems.
